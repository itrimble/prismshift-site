Prism Shift collects no data.

Updated 8 October 2026

Your play stays on your device

Prism Shift does not collect or transmit your gameplay, progress, achievements, settings or personal identifiers to the developer. The game has no accounts, no advertising, no tracking and no analytics service.

What the game saves

Controls, audio and accessibility preferences, tutorial history, unlocked levels, local achievements and your Abyss high score are saved on your device. These records support play and are not sent to a developer-operated service.

Sound and controls

The soundtrack and sound effects run on your device. The game does not record your microphone or camera. Touch input and optional game-controller input are used only to control the game.

Apple services

Apple may handle App Store transactions, device backups and diagnostics according to your Apple settings and Apple's privacy policy. Prism Shift does not operate those services and does not receive a gameplay analytics feed from them.

Removing local data

Deleting the app removes its local game records. Restoring a device backup may restore saved records. Tutorial history can also be reset from Settings.

Children

Prism Shift is suitable for all ages. Because the game collects nothing and has no accounts, chat or advertising, there is no personal information to gather from anyone, children included.

This website

This page is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics. Fonts are requested from Google Fonts, which receives the request as part of serving them. GitHub may log requests as part of operating the service.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it. Questions about privacy: email iantrimble@remnantsecurity.com with “Prism Shift privacy” in the subject.
