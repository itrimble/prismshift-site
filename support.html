Help with Prism Shift

Build the light. Break the darkness.

How do I play?

Connect three or more crystals of one colour. They do not clear straight away — they charge, and wait. The Pulse Line sweeps the board in time with the music and ignites everything you have built. Bank several charged networks for a single sweep and their energy multiplies.

A dark tide rises beneath the board. Igniting light at its edge pushes it back and reclaims room to build, so counter-play is always available when things get tight.

Controls

  • Drag to move a piece.
  • Tap to rotate it.
  • Drag downward to lower it.
  • Flick deliberately to commit it, so nothing drops by accident.

Prefer buttons? Optional on-screen button and hybrid control schemes are in Settings, and compatible game controllers work on iPhone, iPad and Apple TV.

Accessibility

  • Gentle Tide slows the rising dark tide so you can play at your own pace.
  • Reduce Motion calms cascades, sweeps and background motion.
  • Three graphics modes — standard, cel-shaded and premium crystal.
  • Music and sound effects have separate volume controls, or mute them and play your own music.

None of these lock content away. The full campaign stays open whichever you choose.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, on your device. Unlocked levels, local achievements, your Abyss high score and all your preferences are stored locally. There is no account to create and nothing is uploaded. Deleting the app removes those records; restoring a device backup may bring them back.

Something went wrong

Email iantrimble@remnantsecurity.com with “Prism Shift” in the subject. Include your device model, iOS or tvOS version, and what happened. Please do not include passwords or payment information — they are never needed for support.

Privacy

Prism Shift collects nothing. The full policy is on the privacy page.
